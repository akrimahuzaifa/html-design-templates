Major Changes Deep Dive

1. Climate & Biodiversity (4.1)

Context analysis now requires documented consideration of environmental dependencies.

Example: A manufacturer must now evaluate how local biodiversity loss might impact water supply or how climate volatility affects their specific logistics chain.

2. Lifecycle Perspective (4.3)

The scope must extend beyond the "factory gates" to include influence over the entire value chain.

Example: You may need to assess supplier emissions and the environmental impact of product disposal at end-of-life.

3. Change Management (6.3)

A new mandatory clause for structured management of EMS-related changes, similar to traditional MOC (Management of Change) processes.

4. Supplier Controls (8.1)

Documented influence over "externally provided processes" is no longer optional—it is a core performance metric.

Transition FAQ

Q: How much work is this compared to the 2015 transition?
A: While not a total structural overhaul, the technical depth regarding "Lifecycle" and "Change Management" is significantly higher. Expect a moderate effort for documentation updates.

Q: What happens if we don't transition in time?
A: Beyond losing your certificate, you risk failing customer audits and being disqualified from tenders that require the "latest" environmental standards.
