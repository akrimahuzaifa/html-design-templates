Roadmap to Clinical Success

Our expert advice for excelling during your rotations.

01

Always Read!

Utilize "pocket" books and handbooks to stay sharp on clinical protocols daily.

02

Stay Prepared

Carry your white coat, stethoscope, penlight, and reflex hammer at all times.

03

Professionalism

Dress the part. Minimize accessories to maintain a professional hospital appearance.

04

Master Note-Taking

Document everything. This is crucial for USMLE prep and residency applications.

05

Follow Regulations

Adhere strictly to hospital rules and wear your ID tag prominently.

06

Positive Mindset

Clinical rotations are demanding. Stay emotionally and mentally resilient.
