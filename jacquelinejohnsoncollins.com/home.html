If This Is You...

You’ve survived things most people can’t imagine. You managed the appointments, the adjustments, and the hard days while holding everything together.

But somewhere in all that surviving, you lost track of you. You find yourself wondering: Who am I now, outside of this diagnosis — or this disruption?

Why does it feel like I’m still just enduring, even on the good days? Is it too late to feel like myself again?

It is not too late. You are reclaiming what was always yours.

Yes — This Is Me. Let’s Talk.

I know this road. I have walked it.

I’m Jacqueline Johnson-Collins, licensed mental health therapist, social worker, published author, and transformational coach.

In 1997, I was diagnosed with MS and told I would be in a wheelchair within ten years. I refused that prognosis. Now I help successful midlife women do the meaningful work of reclaiming who they are on the other side of disruption.
